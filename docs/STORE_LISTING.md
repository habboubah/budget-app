### 2.3 Graphics

| Asset | What Google wants | Where it is |
|---|---|---|
| App icon | 512 × 512, 32-bit PNG, at most 1024 KB | `screenshots/store/play-icon-512.png` (from `assets/icon-only.png`) |
| Feature graphic | 1024 × 500, JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha | `screenshots/store/play-feature/<language>/feature-graphic.jpg` (`npm run store-screenshots`), with the *Kostenlos* / *Free* ribbon and the four points |
| Phone screenshots | 2 to 8; JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha; each side 320–3840 px; the long side at most twice the short one (1080 × 1920 is the usual size) | `screenshots/store/play-phone/<language>/` — eight slides, 1080 × 1920 JPEG, Android frame, the *Kostenlos* / *Free* ribbon on each (`npm run store-screenshots`; the `play-phone` target in the studio's config) |
| Tablet screenshots, video | Optional | Leave empty |

The ten screens, in store order: month · new entry · backup · budgets · next
month · statistics · history · category · month report · Planen. Play takes
eight: history and category drop out, and backup comes second.

The backup slide is the promise: *Deine Daten bleiben bei dir* — no account,
no cloud, shared with no one — with the points *Keine Werbung · Keine
In-App-Käufe · Kein Abo · Nichts verlässt dein Handy*. The feature graphic
carries the same four points. On Play every slide also has the *Kostenlos* /
*Free* ribbon in the corner. Google's guidelines ask to avoid "Free" on
preview assets: the listing stays up, but Play promotes and recommends such
assets less readily. The App Store set has neither the ribbon nor the points
about the price (Apple rejects price words on screenshots, guideline 2.3.7);
it keeps *Keine Werbung* and *Nichts verlässt dein Handy*. All of it is only
true while Haushaltsbuch Einfach costs nothing: with a paid Plus the ribbon and the price
points come off (`.claude/skills/store-screenshot-studio/reference/store-specs.md`).

### 2.5 Data safety

**Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** No.

Nothing leaves the device: no server, no analytics, no ads, no Google
services. Photos for receipts and for reading a statement, the fingerprint or
face unlock, reminders and the automatic backups all stay on the phone. The
device backup is Android's own and the user's choice (*Im Gerätebackup
sichern*); Google counts it as the user's backup, not as collection by the app.

- **Is all of the user data encrypted in transit?** Not applicable — none is sent.
- **Do you provide a way to request data deletion?** Not applicable — no account; *Optionen › Alle Daten löschen* erases everything on the device.

1) Screen recording
Attached: a screen recording from an iPhone [model] on iOS [version]. It starts with launching the app and shows every feature: setting up the first month, adding, splitting and ticking off entries, the Excel import, the bank CSV import, reading transactions from a photo, statistics, planning, settings, backup and restore, reminders and the Face ID lock. All names and amounts in it are invented.

2) Purpose and target audience
Haushaltsbuch Einfach is a household budget book for private individuals and families who keep track of their monthly income and expenses, as a replacement for a monthly spreadsheet. Every entry is either paid or still open, and the app shows two balances: what is in the account now, and what will be left at the end of the month once everything open is paid.

3) How to use the app
No login, account or sample files are needed. To see every screen filled in: Settings > Try it out > Add test data (a year of invented household data; Remove test data takes it out again).
- Month tab: + adds an entry (amount, name, category, optional due day and monthly/yearly repeat). Tap the circle in front of an entry to mark it as paid; both balances update. Recurring entries come along into the next month as open.
- Transactions without typing, all read on the device and shown for checking before anything is saved: Month > ... > Import bank transactions (CSV) takes the file from online banking; Month > ... > Take transactions from a photo reads a photo or screenshot of a banking app; Settings > Back up and transfer > Import from Excel reads a monthly budget spreadsheet. The recording shows all three with demo files.
- Statistics tab: what stands out, income and spending over time, spending by category or payee.
- Plan tab: the month so far, budgets, a twelve-month outlook, savings pots, subscriptions and contracts, a money check.
- Settings: language, appearance, currency, categories and budgets, backup export (encrypted with a password by default) and import, automatic backups on the device, Face ID lock, reminders, delete all data.

Permissions, each asked only when the user turns the feature on:
- Camera and photo library: only to read a transaction list from a picture the user takes or picks.
- Save to photos: only if the user saves the year-in-review picture from the share sheet.
- Face ID: optional app lock (Apple's LocalAuthentication, with the device passcode as fallback).
- Notifications: optional local reminders of open bills and contract notice dates.

4) External services
None. The app makes no network requests: no server, no account or authentication service, no analytics, no ads, no payment processor, no AI service, no bank connection or data provider. Text in pictures is read on the device by tesseract.js, an open-source text recognition library bundled with the app together with its language data; the picture is not saved or sent anywhere. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. All data stays on the device; an exported backup goes only where the user saves it through the iOS share sheet.

5) Regional differences
None. The app works the same in all regions. The interface is available in 14 languages (German, English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Turkish) and follows the device language. The currency is suggested from the device region and only changes the symbol shown; nothing is converted.

6) Regulated industry / third-party material
Not applicable. Haushaltsbuch Einfach is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not connect to banks, move or hold money, offer financial products or give personal financial advice; the money check shows general rules of thumb and explains how each figure is worked out. It contains no protected third-party material.

Thank you for your time.
